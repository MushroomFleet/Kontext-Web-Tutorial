Intermediate #43

Multi-Character Scene Combination

Master complex scene composition by combining multiple subjects from different sources into a cohesive environment. This technique demonstrates advanced image integration skills including natural lighting harmony, spatial relationships, and unified environmental context.

Technique Overview

Multi-character scene creation represents one of FLUX Kontext's most sophisticated capabilities. This technique requires understanding of spatial relationships, lighting consistency, and environmental harmony to create believable composite scenes. Key challenges include:

  • • Spatial coordination - Characters must be positioned naturally within shared space
  • • Lighting unification - All subjects must appear under consistent lighting conditions
  • • Scale relationships - Character sizes must be proportionally correct for shared environment
  • • Environmental integration - Background elements must support and unify multiple subjects

This approach is essential for storytelling imagery, creating family portraits from separate photos, marketing campaigns featuring multiple subjects, and fantasy scene composition.

FLUX Kontext Prompt

Instruction-Based Editing Prompt
Place woman and bunny in the same scene

Why This Prompt Works

This prompt uses straightforward language to request complex scene integration. "Place woman and bunny in the same scene" clearly identifies both subjects and requests their unification into a shared environment. The simplicity allows FLUX Kontext to intelligently determine appropriate spatial relationships, lighting, and environmental context for both characters.
